One store. One order.

Small orders and institution orders run through the same catalogue — you just tell it which one you are doing. Switch to bulk, name the institution and the number of students, then set a per-student quantity on any card. It multiplies through, applies the volume tier, and drops the whole package into your cart as a single line.
