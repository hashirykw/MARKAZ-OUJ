White belt to black, in ten grades.

Taekwondo grades are tested by our own panel three times a year. Nobody is promoted on attendance — you go up when the panel says the form is there. Boxing, kickboxing and MMA have no belt ladder; progress there is measured in rounds.

Who will be teaching you.

Everyone on this floor is on our staff, police-verified, and has competed in what they teach. Open a name to see their grade and what they run.

Fees, in plain numbers.

No admission fee, no annual charge, no minimum term. Uniform and grading are billed separately, only when you need them.

Second sibling and onward: 15% off. School and corporate programmes are quoted per group — call and ask for the programme desk.

Come and train once, for free.

Turn up in loose clothes. We lend you the pads. If it is not for you, that is the end of it — there is nothing to cancel and nobody will call you.
